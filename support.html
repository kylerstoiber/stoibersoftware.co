Fliqit

Fliqit has its own help page at getfliqit.com/support.html — start there. The basics:

How it works

  1. Screenshot anything with a date in it — a text, a flyer, an email, a schedule.
  2. Tap Share and pick Fliqit.
  3. Check the title, date, time, and location, then approve it. Only what you approve goes on your calendar.

Fliqit isn’t in my share sheet

Scroll the share sheet’s row of apps to the end, tap More, then Edit, and switch Fliqit on. Drag it toward the front so it’s always handy.

SaleScout

Nothing shows up near me

Make sure location access is on (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → SaleScout), then zoom out on the map or loosen your filters. Some areas are quiet mid-week; check the sale calendar for what’s coming up this weekend.

Saved-search alerts aren’t arriving

Alerts need notification permission: Settings → Notifications → SaleScout. Then open the saved search in the app and make sure alerts are switched on for it.

A sale is wrong, over, or shouldn’t be listed

Email us with the sale’s name and location and we’ll fix or remove it.

Signing in, and deleting my account

SaleScout uses Sign in with Apple or Google — there’s no separate password to remember. To delete your account and everything stored with it, go to Profile → Delete Account in the app. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account.

Badges and levels

Using SaleScout moves you up the ranks, from Bin Digger all the way to The Holy Grail. Progress is stored with your account, so it follows you to a new phone.

SaleScout Pro subscription

  • Manage or cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SaleScout. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the current period ends.
  • Free trial: the trial turns into a paid subscription unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • New phone? Open SaleScout, go to Settings → Restore Purchases, and make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds: Apple handles billing, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
