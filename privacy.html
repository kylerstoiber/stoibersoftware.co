Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026 · Stoiber Software LLC

This policy covers the apps published by Stoiber Software LLC: Fliqit and SaleScout. The short version: the apps do their work on your phone wherever they can, there’s no account to create, and we never sell your data.

What applies to every app

  • No accounts. You don’t sign up for anything, and we don’t ask for your name, email address, or contacts.
  • No selling data. We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.
  • No third-party ads. There are no ad networks in our apps.
  • Purchases go through Apple. Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled by the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple’s privacy policy covers that transaction.
  • Diagnostics. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We can’t identify you from them. We don’t use any other analytics or crash-reporting service.

Fliqit

Fliqit turns a screenshot into a calendar event. It only ever sees a screenshot when you deliberately share one to it from the iOS share sheet.

What it uses

  • The screenshot you share. Fliqit reads the text in the image to find a title, date, time, and location. All of this happens on your device using Apple’s built-in text recognition. The image is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Calendar access. With your permission, Fliqit adds the event you approve to your calendar. It doesn’t read, change, or upload your existing events.

What it keeps

Nothing on our end. Fliqit doesn’t store the screenshot after the event is created, and the event lives in your calendar, where you control it.

SaleScout

SaleScout shows yard sales, estate sales, and thrift stores near you.

What it uses

  • Location. With your permission, SaleScout uses your location to center the map and find sales nearby. To fetch listings, the app sends the map area you’re looking at to our servers; it doesn’t send a history of where you’ve been. You can use the app without location access by moving the map yourself. Location is only used while the app is open.
  • Notifications. If you turn on saved-search alerts, the app asks for notification permission so it can tell you when a matching sale appears. Your saved searches are stored on your device and checked from there.
  • Your saves. Saved searches, favorites, pinned locations, and badge progress are stored on your device and not synced anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • SaleScout Pro. The subscription is billed and managed by Apple. We receive an anonymous receipt that says a subscription is active — not your name or card details.
  • Maps. The map itself is provided by Apple Maps, which receives the area you view under its own privacy policy.

Listings

Sale listings come from publicly posted sources. If a listing includes information about you that you’d like removed, email us and we’ll take care of it.

Your choices

  • Turn location, calendar, or notification access off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. The related feature stops working; the rest of the app keeps going.
  • Deleting an app deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • Want to know what, if anything, we hold about you, or have it deleted? Email us. We’ll reply within 30 days.

Children

Our apps aren’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Meaningful changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
