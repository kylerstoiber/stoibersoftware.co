SaleScout

iOS · Shopping · On the App Store

Never miss a sale again.

Every garage sale, estate sale, and thrift store near you, on one map. Made for weekend bargain hunters, vintage collectors, estate-sale regulars, and furniture flippers.

  • Live map of nearby sales, color-coded by type, with clustering when it gets busy.
  • Photo-rich listings gathered from public sources and refreshed every few hours.
  • Saved-search alerts the moment a matching sale is posted.
  • Favorites and a route builder that hands off to Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.
  • Badges that level you up from Bin Digger to The Holy Grail.

Free to download. SaleScout Pro unlocks the full toolkit — monthly or yearly, with a free trial. Built with React Native and Expo on a Supabase backend.

Fliqit

iOS · Productivity · Coming soon

Click it. Fliq it. Plan it.

Any screenshot or photo with a date becomes a calendar event. Fliqit reads it — you approve it.

  • No typing. Dates, times, and places are read straight from the image — texts, flyers, emails, schedules, reservations.
  • Lives in the share sheet. Screenshot, tap Share, pick Fliqit. One Fliq, however many events it holds.
  • You approve it. Nothing lands on your calendar without your OK.
  • Google or Apple Calendar. Pick where your events should go.

Free to start with 5 Fliqs. A native SwiftUI app with a Share Extension. More at getfliqit.com.
