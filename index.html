About

One person. Two apps. Western Springs, Illinois.

Hi, I’m Kyler. I study Information Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with a minor in Computer Science and a soft spot for data science and marketing analytics. On the side, I build iOS apps.

Stoiber Software LLC is the company I started in August 2026 to publish them. It’s a one-person shop: I do the design, the code, and the marketing. The goal is simple — small apps that solve a real, everyday problem and feel good to use.

The company is based in Western Springs, Illinois, a little west of Chicago.
